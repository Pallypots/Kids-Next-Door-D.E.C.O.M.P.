# The Xbox shader, light, font and image databases (subsystem `at/xshade`)

Worktree branch, for the integrator. State: Semantic-tested. No byte match is claimed: the host compiler is not the original's. Names are inferred. Started from the Xbox texture and material branch (`worktree-agent-a25cff59be0e40cf0`, commit 0611475), with `master` merged in (`--no-ff`, commit 310d2d0) for the image and font bodies (`at/imgfont`), which that branch did not have; the merge was clean, but for one line it broke in `tests/native/test_xdb.cpp` (`AtIoBinaryFile::s_context`, folded into `AtAudioBankFile` on master), fixed here.

## What a green result does not show, first

What a 2D font or an image does with the graphics device once read (the font's slot 9, 0x00123850; the image's slot 10, 0x00124CD0) is the seam, `src/at/AtGrD3DItemSeam.cpp`, and in the oracle it is the harness's on both sides. The doubles mark each item as loaded in a way emptying and destroying can see: a handle in the item's own 2D surface, now and then blocks for that surface to free, the item's own texture database put on the owner's list, and for a font now and then the flag that makes emptying forget its character table. Nothing of pixels, glyphs or drawing is compared. A shader and a light have nothing of the device: everything they hold is compared.

## Oracle

Side 0 is the original's concrete classes throughout (its constructors 0x00110260, 0x001111B0, 0x001115A0, 0x00111BC0, so its own vftables and items) and the bases' `Load` (0x0002B2E0, 0x0002AC10, 0x0002A290, 0x00029DC0); side 1 the recovered. The device's release (0x00205D10) and the two seams are logging doubles on both sides.

Cases: every distinct `.AGS`, `.AGL`, `.AGF` and `.AGI` in the cases: each loaded, its items compared field by field (shaders' staged and programmed components byte for byte, lights whole, fonts' and images' every field, character tables, their own texture databases and surfaces), the accessor at three indices, loaded again over itself (shaders and lights; for fonts and images see below), cleared twice, loaded after clearing, destroyed through slot 0. For fonts and images also each of the first three items' `FreeData` with the owning flag set and not, and `Free`; for lights a `Reset` through the original's table. Written texts for each kind: shaders with no components, staged components finding a texture and not, a name space and a programmed component; lights with every word, several, a name space; fonts with a character set, demand loading, scale, leading, a name space and material assignments; images with every source, demand loading, a transparent colour and a name space. ## Quirks of the original, kept

* A font database's and an image database's `Clear` zero `m_next`, unlike the texture, material and mesh databases'. The font database frees its binary block, goes off the owner's list, then deletes its fonts; the image database deletes its images first, then goes off the list.
* A font's `FreeData` resets its surface and then clears its texture database; an image's does the reverse.
* A 2D font's `Free` forgets its character table rather than freeing it when flag 0x200 is set (`kBorrowedChars`; what sets it is not recovered, probably the binary form), and leaves the base's flags, count and scales, as `AtGrI2DFont::Free` does.
* `MakeLights` keeps the owner after making the array, on the path where the array could not be made too.
* `ShaderAt`, `LightAt`, `Font2DAt` and `ImageAt` are not bounds checked. The shader and light databases are on no owner's list.
* `AtLight`'s constructor, destructor and `Reset` store the same values: white, a range of 1000, attenuation 1, 0, 0, no owner. `Reset` alone leaves the table.
* The concrete shader and light destructors are jumps to their bases' (0x00110250, 0x000EBFD0).
