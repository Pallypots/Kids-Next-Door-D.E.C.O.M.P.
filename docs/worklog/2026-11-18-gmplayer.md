## What a real build does with this

Still nothing reaches it from data: no creator walks the static list of create functions (FaAcPlayer's factory 0x000948E0 is one of them). Called directly, a real build constructs, shuts down and destroys a GmAcPlayer subclass and a FaAcPlayer completely, including the shared segment query of the sight queries, makes FaAcPlayers singly and in arrays, and answers their small slots, slot 44, the turn test (61), the look limits (63, 64), the controller frame and the partner. Where it stops:

* Any slot 2 (record read) of either class reaches the registry's search, which is a seam (0x000679E0, "Gm character: ... is not recovered"): GmAcPlayer's slot 2 does not test for a record before reading. On this branch nothing sets `GmDesignDefaults::s_instance` either.
* FaAcPlayer's `ReadLook` (slot 65) and `MoveFrame` (slot 60) stop at the input's axis value (0x0006DFE0, "Gm player: ..."); `test_gmplayer --seam` checks it stops. On this branch nothing sets `GmPlayerApp::s_instance` (App) either, so a real build would crash reading it before the seam.
* GmAcPlayer's slot 20 with a partner stops at the node's slot 27 (0x0014C170, not recovered), which it calls directly; slots 4, 10, 20 then go to the character's, which reach the actor's unrecovered slots 4, 10, 20.
* FaAcPlayer's slots 15 (LevelContinuation, Combat, Target), 20 (the update, 691 bytes) and 21 (shooting) stop with diagnostics, as do the target sprite's two set-up slots.

## Quirks kept

* GmAcPlayer's slot 2 reads its two words with no test for a record. Its slot 20 calls the node's slot 27, not its own (which answers 1). SetPartner tells a partner let go of to drop its controller only when no new partner comes, and tests the flag every character sets.
* Slot 44 dereferences the +0x304 object without a test, for kind 1 and kind 0 with names.
* The controller frame calls the owner's slot 65 before changing; its destructor makes it new again. The forwarders 0x00131A40 and 0x00131A70 do not test the controller; 0x00131A50 and 0x00131A80 do. ReadLook does not test it either.
* FaAcPlayer's Reset clears the combat object's pointer without deleting it; the shutdown deletes it and leaves the pointer for Reset. The destructor is the shutdown inlined. +0x3C0 to +0x3D4 and +0x4B4 are never reset.
* FaAcPlayer's slot 2 copies PowuhUp's name as at most eight characters before looking for the record, only when it has a character, and leaves the powuh words as they were when not found.
* MoveFrame zeroes an axis whose size is below MinMoveStickMagnitude (0.2 without one), tests the square root of the sum of squares against zero, scales right by turning times its size times 1.5 (kept in the x87's precision; double here) and forward by the forward axis negated. With no stick it hands out the node's frame and answers 0.
* The sight query's destructor sets three of its words again after counting itself out.
* FindRecord measures the whole name only to test it for zero.

## Not compared, and why

* GmAcPlayer's own slot 65: `__purecall`, which would end the process on the original side.
* MoveFrame's scale: the products with each component are rounded twice on both sides, to 64 bits on the original and to 53 here, then to a float; the cases show no difference, but a tie at the second rounding could differ. The sum of squares is compared in double, which is above zero exactly when the x87's extended sum is.
