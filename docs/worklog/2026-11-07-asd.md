# Worklog: scene lists (.ASD), cell graphs (.ASC), flat images (.ASI) and text (.AST) (2026-11-07)

For the integrator: this holds what belongs in docs/formats.md, docs/verification.md and docs/portability.md. State of this work: Semantic-tested. A host MATCH from `tools/verify.py` says nothing about the original compiler.

## What the formats are

Established from the keyword tables at 0x002B13A0 (11 words), 0x002B2120 (12), 0x002B18A8 (11) and 0x002B1910 (14), from what the readers do with them, and from the real files; not from the extensions.

**.ASD: a scene's list of files.** It is to a scene what .AGD is to graphics, and its own word for its kind is the same, `DatabaseLoader`. It holds no data of its own: every word is a list of leaf names, `NodeDatabase[n] { "name" ... }`, and the loader opens each in the directory of the .ASD. So yes, it ties the other scene formats together: for one object or location it names the .ASN nodes, .ASM modifiers, .ASG space graph, .ASB bounding geometry, .ASE emitters, .ASL lens flares, .ASC cell graphs, .AIS strings, .ASI flat images, .AST flat text, and further .ASD lists. Whatever order the file gives, they load in a fixed one: strings, text, images, geometry, modifiers, emitters, lens flares, nodes, cell graphs, space graphs, then nested lists. That order matters: a space graph names modifiers and nodes, which are by then loaded. The owner is the scene owner (`AtNameTableOwner`); the game makes the list at 0x000A1608 and loads "UI\Trans\wipe" with it, and 0x001B4384 loads others.

The 409 real files use six words: NodeDatabase 409, BoundingGeometryDatabase 211, EmitterDatabase 130, SpaceGraph 13, ModifierDatabase 13, CellGraphDatabase 9. Never DatabaseLoader, LensFlareDatabase, StringDatabase, ImageDrawDatabase or TextDrawDatabase. So .ASI and .AST are reached on the disc by no list, and there are none among the text files.

**.ASC: cell graphs**, rooms (cells) and the portals between them, for visibility. See the head of `include/at/AtCellGraph.h` for the grammar. A portal is a polygon in a mesh's vertex array, 3 to 31 vertices; it keeps a pointer into the locked array, the centre of its vertices and an unnormalised normal from the first three; a cell is a run of portals. All 9 real files have one graph each, a Mesh, OppositeCell (never OppositeCellDifferentGraph), Vertices, ClosedByViewerDist, Cell, Portals and ExteriorPortals. What a space graph leaf's `Cell` word finds is a cell of one of these: `AtSpaceCellTable` of the .ASG work **is `AtCellGraph`**, now a typedef.

**.ASI and .AST: flat images and text**, things drawn on the screen, each item a block of words with a position, scale, clip rectangle, four corner colours and an image or a font and string. See `include/at/AtDrawItem.h`.

## Recovered

80 functions, 7 vftables, 14,517 original bytes, in `src/at/AtSceneDatabaseLoader.cpp`, `AtCellGraph.cpp`, `AtDrawItem.cpp`. **This is a third over the budget of 60.** The .ASD closure itself is 31; with the fatal path cut it reaches the .ASC reader (19 more, on real files) and, through vftables, the image and text items (18 small ones, needed for the .ASI and .AST readers the brief names to do anything).

* `AtSceneDatabaseLoader` (0x002749C4): everything but the two walks below.
* `AtCellGraphDatabase` (0x00276148), `AtCellGraph`, `AtCell`, `AtCellPortal`; `AtMesh::VertexArray` 0x00036AD0; owner's `AddCellGraphs` 0x000400F0 and `AddSpaceGraphs` 0x00040130.
* `AtImageDrawDatabase` (0x002752CC), `AtTextDrawDatabase` (0x002752F0), `AtDrawItem` (0x00277108), `AtImageDraw` (0x002764F0), `AtTextDraw` (0x00276504), `AtColorRectangle`; the device's `FindImage` 0x00048E50 and `FindFont` 0x00049290.

## Not recovered

* 0x0002ED70 and 0x0002EDD0, walks over the nodes that the game calls right after loading (they call 0x0003E860 and 0x0003E890 in the scene, and four functions each beyond). Not on the load path.
* Drawing: 0x00048F00, 0x00049330 stop with a diagnostic. Looking through portals: 0x00052E00, 0x000570E0, 0x0005BCE0, 0x0005C6B0.
* A concrete vertex array's lock (slot 22) and a concrete device's scale (slot 25) are the graphics layer's: the recovered base slots stop with a diagnostic.
* 0x00057D80, the base item's deleting destructor: a vftable slot never called. Recorded only as a symbol.

## What a real build does on real files

A recovered build given a real .ASD reads its lists and calls the recovered loaders of the other formats in order, in the .ASD's directory. Those loaders are whatever their branches made; this work tested them only as doubles (see the oracle). A real .ASC reads fully, given a mesh whose vertex arrays lock, which on a real build is the graphics layer, not recovered: there `Lock` stops with a diagnostic. So on a host build today a real .ASD with a CellGraphDatabase stops at the first portal's Vertices.

## Oracle

`python -I tools/oracle_run.py --suite asd --jobs 2`.

**What stands in for what on real files, first:** the seven kinds other branches own (.ASN, .ASM, .ASG, .ASB, .ASE, .ASL, .AIS) are not loaded. On both sides their Load is redirected to a double that logs which object, name, mode, owner and directory it got; their objects are made and destroyed by the real constructors and destructors. Cell graphs, flat lists and nested lists load for real on both sides, on the original's tokenizer against the recovered one. Meshes, vertex arrays (with generated positions), images, fonts and strings are the harness's, found in maps holding every quoted name of a case. So real .ASD files show the lists, names, order, directory and the cell graphs they name agree; not that any node or space graph loads.

Compared: every field of the loader, nested loaders, each list's count cookie, the two maps by name and value, the original's static name lists against the recovered, the visits; every field of every cell graph, cell and portal, reals by bits, pointers by what they lead to; every item field; what the owner's tables find by every name of the case in and out of four name spaces; a log of heap pushes, allocation sizes, frees with their sizes, locks, unlocks, opens and messages. Then destruction and its log.

Cases: all 409 .ASD with all 9 .ASC as their children, and the 9 .ASC alone. Written text for every word and error path of each format, nested lists in three directories, name spaces, files that do not open, empty files, fill 0 and 0xCD, loads twice. Each of six heaps refused after 0 to 13 pushes, and each null. Directly: the three accessors, LoadDatabases with names kept and then freed, the loader's Clear, an item's own Clear, a portal read again with its first flag set.

### Limits and things not run

* Names longer than eight characters, and names that are not strings, read into stack buffers not always terminated: real names fit; not run beyond that.
* After a crash on either side only the logs are compared; the objects are abandoned.
* On a second load of one object only the last load's visits are compared: where the heap puts the new objects is the harness's.
* `AtCellGraph::Read`'s test for an earlier read cannot be reached through a loader. See the suite for the mutants dropped as equivalent and why.

## Quirks of the original worth keeping

* **A graph can name only graphs before it in its own file.** Each graph is put in the map just before its body is read, and its portals are resolved at the end of its body; a later graph is not there yet, and the lookup stops.
* **A second ImageDraw or TextDraw list in one file destroys itself.** It makes a new array, leaking the first's; then `GmNameMap::Init` calls the list's own virtual Clear, which frees the new array and zeroes the count; the rest of the list is reported word by word, and the list ends empty.
* An .ASD list given twice reports, then makes a new array of the new count without freeing the first; the names list is replaced likewise.
* The loader's third argument is passed unchanged as every child's "mode", which every child ignores. 0x0002F7F0 reuses the slot of its name argument for the file system it pushed the directory on.
* The nested loaders' names are the loader's own, where all other lists are shared statics: a nested loader overwrites the shared lists, safe only because they were freed first.
* The strings' Load gets the owner's word at +0x0C as an `AtIoContext`, and a text item's String word looks in that context's default group. On the sound owner, which shares the layout, that word is the bank loader.
* A portal's vertex array stays locked from one portal to the next, and is unlocked at the end of the graph; the lock record is not cleared after the unlock, but is at the start of the next graph.
* A portal without Vertices reads through a null pointer when its normal is computed.
* The centre of a portal's vertices: the compiler's unrolling by four keeps some of the three sums in x87 registers across some additions, so which are rounded to float, and when, is in the result's low bits. `ComputeCenter` writes that out.
* `ClosedByViewerDist` keeps the square. `Closed` sets two bits, 2 and 8.
* An image's position is not scaled by the device; text's is. Clip rectangles are, for both.
* The device has two more name tables than were known, of fonts (+0x258) and images (+0x294), each with its name spaces.

## Mistakes along the way

* **Over budget**, by a third: see Recovered.
* My first written cell graph named a later graph in its own file, and every case using it crashed in the portal lookup on both sides, so it compared only logs and looked green. A count of guarded crashes per case showed it; the case is now a quirk of its own.
* My first image list had two lists, so it ended empty on both sides, and 9 image-word mutants and the list's name mutant survived. One list now, and the two-list case apart.
* The harness's own maps gave one object under every table and name space, so looking in the wrong table was invisible (3 survivors). Each table now finds a different object.
* A harness `std::string` freed after logging was turned back on, logging a free on one side only.
* Two mutants changed an enum value into one already used and did not compile, which stopped the runner.
* First full run 350 / 382. Gaps closed by cases; 15 dropped as equivalent with reasons in the suite. Two of those are a gap in the harness, not equivalence: the order of three same-sized name arrays' allocation and freeing.

## Portability notes

* `AtCellPortal::m_vertices` points into a locked vertex array, a host pointer. `AtVertexLock` keeps 0x48 bytes of room past its two known words, for whatever the graphics layer's lock writes there.
* Counts ahead of arrays are `size_t`, through `AtSceneArray.h`.
