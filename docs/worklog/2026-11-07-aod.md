# Sound databases: `.AOD`, `.AOE` (subsystem `at/aod`)

State: Semantic-tested. No byte match is claimed: the host compiler is not the original's. Names are inferred, except `AtSoXboxEmitter` and `AtSoXboxXactBank`, which the original's own messages use.

## What the formats are

Established from the code, its keyword tables, its messages and the text:

* An `.AOD` file names other files, relative to its own directory: `BankDatabase[n] { "name" ... }` (`.AOB`), `SegmentDatabase[n]` (`.AOS`), `EmitterDatabase[n]` (`.AOE`), `DatabaseLoader[n]` (another `.AOD`). No file on the disc uses `DatabaseLoader`. Only `AUDIO\GLOBAL.AOD` uses `EmitterDatabase`.
* An `.AOE` file is a list of emitters: `Emitter[n] { Emitter "name" { ... } ... }`, with the words `Center`, `Stereo`, `Emitter3D`, `Position x y z`, `Orientation x y z w`, and `NameSpace` before the block. An emitter is a named place a sound comes from: an `AtCoordSys` with a kind and perhaps an SDK stream. `Emitter3D` makes a stream of kind 2. `Center` makes a stream of kind 1 on mix bin 2, which is the front centre. `Stereo` and nothing make no stream. There is one real file, `AUDIO\EMITTERS.AOE`, with 50 emitters: `sfx`, `ui`, `voice`, `music`, `ambience`, `collsn1-5`, `actor1-40`.
* Position and orientation are real numbers. An integer there is reported as "expected real".

A finding the earlier notes did not have: 38 list files lie **loose on the disc**, under `/DataXbox/Audio/Music/`, in no archive, beside their `.wba` files: 8 `.aod`, 15 `.aob`, 15 `.AOS`. The text cases come only from the archives, so they lack these, and `AUDIO\GLOBAL.AOD` names four of them. `tests/oracle/aod_loose_cases.py` writes them to `analysis-local/scratch/aod/loose`, and the oracle will not run without them. To regenerate: `python -I tests/oracle/aod_loose_cases.py "<disc image>" analysis-local/scratch/aod/loose`, from the main checkout. The integrator may prefer this in `tools/jam.py textcases`; I was not to edit that. The `aob` suite has not seen these 30 lists either.

## Does a real `.AOD` bring in its banks and segments?

Yes, as far as the bank's data file, and then a real build stops. Precisely:

`AtAudioDatabase::Load` reads the names, and each list is loaded by the recovered readers into the recovered concrete lists: `AtSoXboxBankDatabase`, `AtSoXboxSegmentDatabase`, `AtSoXboxEmitterDatabase`. Every `Bank` entry on the disc is `PlatformSource` with no residence, so every one becomes an `AtSoXboxXactBank`. Every segment finds its bank: 1,787 of 1,787.

The stop is in the bank. `OnInit` calls the bank's load at once unless the entry says `DelayLoad`, and that load opens `<name>.WBA`, and its next step (0x0011F6F0, messages `eUpdateLoad`) parses an XACT sound bank and speaks to XACT. Those are outside the scope, so `src/at/AtSoXboxHost.cpp` answers `AtSoXboxXactBankLoad` with a diagnostic. **So a real build, on a real `.AOD`, stops at the first bank without `DelayLoad`**, which is the first bank of nearly every file. With the seam supplied, as the oracle and the host test do, the whole file loads. Likewise the emitters' SDK calls.

Also not reached by a real build: what makes the database (0x000E9E90, a slot of the sound system class, called by the `.GOD` loader 0x0013AD70) is not recovered, so nothing in the game's flow calls this yet.

## `AddScene` 0x00031A90 and the table at +0x90

Not renamed; reported as asked. **"Scene" is not the right name for that method or that table.** `AtAudioEmitterDatabase::Read` 0x00025360 calls it with an emitter list: a name map whose values are emitters, not scene nodes. Evidence that the table really serves that:

* 0x00137660 loads the owner from 0x003B3558, adds 0x90, and calls `GmNameMapGroup::Find` 0x00017ED0 for the literal names `ui`, `sfx`, `voice`, `music`, `ambience`, then `collsn%d`, `actor%d`: exactly the names in `EMITTERS.AOE`. 16 sites read through that global at +0x90 or +0xA8.
* 0x000B0910 and 0x000B0DA3 find `voice` the same way, and read +0x28 and +0x40 of the result, which are an emitter's owner and voice list.
* The `.GOD` loader passes that same global to `.AOD` loading as the owner (0x0013AEED).

So one class, `AtNameTableOwner`, has its third table holding emitter lists on the sound owner and scenes on the scene owner. I did not establish whether those are ever one object; emitters are `AtCoordSys`, not `AtSceneNode`, so `FindNode` 0x00045680 would miscast one if they were. Suggested name, in the pattern of its neighbours: `AddToThird`, with `m_third` and `m_thirdSpaces`; `FindNode` is fine as a name for its own callers.

Similarly, for the `aob` header: `AtAudioBank` slots 10 and 15 are named `Unload` and `Update`. On the concrete bank, the original's messages call slot 10 `eUpdateLoad` and slot 15 `vUnload`, and slot 13 `vInitPlatformSpecific`. The base's names look wrong. Not renamed.

`AtAudioBankDesc::kWba` is 3, and `PlatformSource` being 3 means it: the bank opens with the extension `WBA` (0x414257 at 0x0011F4C3).

## Quirks kept

* An `.AOD`'s lists of names are statics, 0x002C6280-0x002C628C. An `.AOD` named by an `.AOD` frees the outer one's: with two under `DatabaseLoader` the first frees the table the second is read from, and both sides crash. One works. The disc has neither.
* Repeating a word reports code 0x27, and if the report returns, the new list replaces the old, which is lost.
* An unknown word reports, then still expects a right curly.
* Banks' slot 0 makes nothing for a count of zero; the other three allocate anyway. Banks' index slot gives null past the end; the other three do not check.
* `Load` clears only if it has an owner, so counts with no owner stay, and go through null lists.
* `AtSoXboxBankDatabase::CreateBank` makes no bank, with no report, for anything but `PlatformSource` with no residence. `DiskResident` on an Xbox bank means no bank.
* The `.AOE` reader stops after its first `Emitter` block. Reading again after a count of zero crashes: `Init` clears `m_owner` through the slot, and the add goes through the member, not the argument. I first wrote the argument, and the oracle found it.
* `NameSpace` chooses the table's group only. The emitter's own copy of it goes in a desc nothing reads, along with +0x18.
* `AtSoXboxXactBank::ClearFields` copies 0x118 bytes from an uninitialised local, with only the first byte and the last three words set. `OnInit` makes a desc it does not use.
* `AtSoXboxXactBank` does not call the base's `Load`, so it never reaches the owner's loader through it.
* A failed bank file open in the original writes its message to a local and discards it (0x0011F525); noted from reading, not run.

Every `.AOD`: 150 distinct from the archives and 8 loose, each loaded, over itself, cleared twice, loaded again and destroyed, through the original's whole chain on one side and the recovered chain on the other. That is real `.AOB`, `.AOS` and `.AOE` files, with paths resolved by the recovered `AtIoPath`. 175 of each list file are there. Every name resolves, and it fails if one does not. Also: 52 written `.AOD` texts at two load statuses, 12 `.AOE` lists alone, segment lists alone, two nested, text left open on a tokenizer that stops it, no memory and no heap at each allocation, the SDK refusing, an emitter and a bank with every field dirty, each format and residence.

Mutant runs: 267/285, then 280/281, then 281/281. The 18 survivors of the first:

* Gaps, now closed: all heaps were one token, so the wrong heap passed (3); no test of the token file's heap gone (1); `Load` clearing unconditionally (1); list `Clear` never compared alone (5); `world` null with an owner never run (1); a stale stream under `Init` never run (1); the base destructor's owner (1); nested clearing order, which needed nested databases loaded one at a time, since two from a file crash (1).
* Dropped as provably equivalent, each with its reason in the suite: the third argument (every list's `Load` ignores it); a segment's `Reset` in the list's `Clear` (it clears only that segment, which is deleted next); `Init`'s first `m_owner` and `m_kind` stores (stored again further down, and nothing between reads them). Before those: the null check before freeing a name, and the `Close` at the end of `.AOE` `Load`, as in `aob`; `space[0] = 0` (only stack bytes in a field nothing reads).

Limits, most important first:

* **The bank's load, process and unload are doubles on both sides**, as are the SDK calls and the matrices. This says the chain reaches them identically, in the same order and with the same state. It says nothing of how a bank loads or plays. No `.WBA` is opened.
* The doubles' load gives each bank five blocks so that `Reset`'s frees show; the real one sets them differently.
* Bytes after a terminator in a name, and most of `Names`, are stack garbage, and not compared.
* Emitter voices are not run. `AtSoBank`'s deleting destructor is reached on the original's side only; on the recovered side it is seen through the derived class.

The "neighbouring class" the `aob` notes mention at 0x00025310 is the emitter list.

`python -I tools/verify.py` on these: 66 functions by the host compiler: 4 MATCH, 2 MISMATCH, 60 SIZE; none MISSING or UNRESOLVED. The 7 vftables MATCH.
